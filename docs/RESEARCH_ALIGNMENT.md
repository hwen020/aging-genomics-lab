# Why this project fits the previously discussed research groups

These are **alignment goals**, not claims that the package is affiliated with any institution.

## USC — Leonard Davis Bioinformatics Core

The target Research Programmer I (#REQ20182636) emphasized human genomic data, GWAS/EWAS/TWAS, differential gene expression, statistics, HPC and documented, reproducible workflows. This starter contributes the **first, narrowly correct expression-analysis capability** and robust software packaging. Future work should prioritize a real aging cohort, DESeq2/R, covariate-adjusted age models, and a separate EWAS module.

References:
- https://gero.usc.edu/research-service-initiatives/cores/bioinformatics-core/
- https://usccareers.usc.edu/en/job/los-angeles/research-programmer-i-1-yr-fixed-term/1209/102948828320

## Stanford — Kundaje Lab, Department of Genetics

The lab works on interpretable models of gene regulation and genetic disease mechanisms at scale. This starter showcases statistical data processing and a testable UI; later extensions can add ENCODE public regulatory features, motif attribution, transcript- or enhancer-level interpretation and scalable reproducible inference.

References:
- https://kundajelab.github.io/
- https://profiles.stanford.edu/anshul-kundaje

## Salk — Integrated Genomics Core

Research software engineering needs reproducible pipelines, well-tested data interfaces, scientific provenance and portable compute. Follow-up work should add Nextflow/Snakemake, containers, Slurm test configuration and storage-conscious streaming.

## Portfolio communication

Say: **"Built an exploratory genomics analysis tool with reproducible data validation, statistical testing, FDR control, PCA, visualization, CLI reports, and regression tests; currently adding a real public RNA-seq case study."**

Do NOT say: "I have performed one year of human genomics research," "I analyzed GWAS/EWAS/TWAS datasets," or "I discovered biological aging biomarkers" unless independently supported by real work.
